For authors

Author guidelines

Manuscripts should be original, unpublished, and not under review elsewhere.

  • Length: 6,000–9,000 words including references and tables.
  • Abstract of up to 250 words with 4–6 keywords.
  • APA (7th edition) referencing style.
  • Anonymised manuscript plus a separate title page with author details.
  • Data and materials statement describing how results can be verified.
Process

Peer review

IJBMR uses double-blind peer review: reviewers and authors do not know each other's identities.

  1. Initial screening

    The editor checks scope, quality and similarity.

  2. Reviewer assignment

    At least two independent subject experts are invited.

  3. Decision

    Accept, minor revision, major revision or reject, always with reasons.

  4. Revision & final check

    Revised papers are re-checked before acceptance.

Integrity

Publication ethics

We follow the principles of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

  • All submissions are screened for plagiarism.
  • Authors, reviewers and editors must declare any conflicts of interest.
  • Research involving people must have appropriate ethical approval and consent.
  • Use of AI tools in writing or analysis must be disclosed.
  • Corrections and retractions are published openly when needed.
Access

Open access & fees

All articles are published under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) licence. Authors keep the copyright to their work.

Fee details (if any) are set by the editorial office. Please contact us for current information and waiver options.
