How it works

From submission to publication

A clear, four-step path. Read the full process on our Standards page.

  1. Submit

    Send your manuscript following our author guidelines.

  2. Editorial check

    An editor checks scope, format and originality.

  3. Peer review

    Two or more reviewers assess the work double-blind.

  4. Publish

    Accepted papers are copy-edited and published open access.
