About IJBMR

We exist to connect careful research with the people who manage organisations every day.

Our mission

Useful, rigorous, open.

The International Journal of Business and Management Research is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal. We publish original research that advances theory and speaks clearly to practice.

We believe good research should be easy to find and easy to read. That's why every article is free, every decision is explained, and every author is treated with respect, whether it's their first paper or their fiftieth.
